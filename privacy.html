Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the PEVOT mobile game, published by Meme Games (Omama Sabbagh) ("PEVOT", "the app", "we", "us"), handles, and why. PEVOT has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Game data stored on your device

To save your progress, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • Best score, coins, shields and unlocked themes and effects
  • Your settings (sound effects on/off, language, selected theme and effect)
  • Whether you bought Remove Ads

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PEVOT is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads (for example, "Watch Ad to Revive"). To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • Device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language)
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city)
  • Ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped)
  • Diagnostic data (for example, crash and performance information related to ads)
  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). If you are in a region where the law requires it, PEVOT shows Google's consent form before any ads are loaded, so you can choose whether your data may be used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the game's start screen.

On iOS, PEVOT asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Google can only use your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) if you allow it. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are less personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Google's handling of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

We only work with third-party services, such as Google AdMob, that provide the same or equal protection of your data as described in this policy.

Remove Ads. If you buy Remove Ads, full-screen (interstitial) ads stop for good, and reviving no longer needs a rewarded ad. PEVOT does not show banner ads.

3. In-app purchases

PEVOT offers one optional purchase, Remove Ads. Payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store (or Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your payment card details, name or billing address. The app only stores, on your device, whether Remove Ads has been bought, and asks the store to confirm or restore it when needed, for example after reinstalling (use Restore Purchases on the start screen).

4. Sharing your score

If you tap Share Score, the app creates a screenshot of your result and opens your device's share sheet. The image is only shared with the app or person you choose. We do not receive it.

5. Children

PEVOT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your choices

  • Change your ad consent with the Privacy button in the game (EEA, UK and Switzerland).
  • Limit ad tracking with the device settings described in section 2.
  • Stop full-screen ads with the one-time Remove Ads purchase.
  • Delete all game data stored on your device by deleting the app.
  • Contact us with any privacy question or request.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Email: meme.gamesx@outlook.com

  • بيانات تشخيصية (مثل معلومات الأعطال والأداء المتعلقة بالإعلانات)

على Android، يمكنك إعادة تعيين معرّف الإعلانات أو حذفه من إعدادات جهازك (الإعدادات ← Google ← الإعلانات، أو الإعدادات ← الخصوصية ← الإعلانات).

تقدّم PEVOT عملية شراء اختيارية واحدة هي إزالة الإعلانات. تتولى App Store من Apple (أو Google Play) معالجة الدفع بالكامل وفق شروطها وسياسات الخصوصية الخاصة بها، ولا نستلم أبداً بيانات بطاقتك أو اسمك أو عنوان الفوترة. يحفظ التطبيق على جهازك فقط ما إذا كنت قد اشتريت «إزالة الإعلانات»، ويطلب من المتجر تأكيد الشراء أو استعادته عند الحاجة، مثلاً بعد إعادة التثبيت (استخدم استعادة المشتريات في الشاشة الرئيسية).
