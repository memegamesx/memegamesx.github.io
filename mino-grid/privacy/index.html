Privacy Policy

MINO Grid · Effective October 14, 2026

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email or an account, and we don't run servers that collect your data.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

This policy explains how MINO Grid ("the app", "the game"), published by Omama Sabbagh ("we", "us"), handles information when you play on iOS, iPadOS or Android, and when you visit this website.

1. Information we do not collect

MINO Grid does not have user accounts. We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information on our own systems. The app does not include analytics or crash-reporting services operated by us.

2. Saved game progress (stored on your device)

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves a small amount of game data in its private storage on your device:

What is savedWhy
Current level, highest level reached, best stars per level and bonus starsTo resume your game and unlock chapters and themes
Chosen tile theme and which tips you have already seenTo keep your preferences and avoid repeating tips
Sound, haptics and language settingsTo remember your settings
Whether you bought "Remove Ads"To keep ads turned off after purchase

This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us and we cannot access it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Depending on your device settings, it may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud or Google backup), which are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policies.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of MINO Grid shows banner, interstitial (full-screen) and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve, limit, measure and prevent fraud in ads, AdMob and its partners may automatically collect information such as:

  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), where permitted
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, taps, video completions) and diagnostic data

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent and choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch, the app shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can accept or decline personalised advertising. If you decline, you may still see ads that are not personalised.
  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your advertising identifier can be used. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but are not based on tracking. You can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Changing your consent: to see the consent form again, clear the app's data or reinstall the app.

Ads in MINO Grid are configured for a general audience. Rewarded videos are always optional and only play when you choose them (for example "Free Star Boost").

Remove Ads

If you buy "Remove Ads", banner and interstitial ads stop being shown. Optional rewarded videos remain available only if you choose to watch them.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive or store your payment card details, name or billing address. The app only stores, on your device, whether "Remove Ads" has been purchased, and asks the store to confirm and restore it when needed (for example after reinstalling).

5. Sharing from the app

If you use a share button, the app opens your device's standard share sheet. What you share and with whom is up to you; we do not receive a copy.

6. Children

MINO Grid is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help address it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. We don't hold any copy to delete. For data collected by Google for advertising, you can manage or delete it through your Google account and device settings described above.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or California law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to object to personalised advertising. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, most requests are best handled through the device and Google settings above, but you can always contact us and we will help.

9. Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by your device's operating system. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we handle to a minimum.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email omama.games@outlook.com.
